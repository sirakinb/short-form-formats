# Posting checklist

- [ ] 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264, audio at -14 LUFS (`tools/loudnorm.sh`).
- [ ] A 720p copy reviewed on your phone (`tools/phone_copy.sh`).
- [ ] A contact sheet checked (`tools/contact_sheet.py`): nothing in the top or bottom 240 px, no animation cut off early.
- [ ] Every claim matches the product's own site or docs.
- [ ] No banned phrases from `brand.json`.
- [ ] No real person's likeness without permission.
- [ ] Cover checked in the 3:4 crop.
- [ ] Caption: 2–5 sentences, ends on a question, 8–12 hashtags, your CTA.
- [ ] "Comment WORD" CTA only if DM automation is live.
- [ ] AI-content label on for avatars, AI characters and AI video.
