# Testing and stats

## Instagram: trial reels
1. Make 2–3 versions of a reel that differ only in the first 3 seconds and the cover.
2. Post them as trial reels (shown to non-followers first).
3. After 48–72 hours, share the winner to your followers and note what won.

## TikTok: carousels
Post, then after 48 hours record plays, likes, saves, shares and comments.

## The stats log
Keep one table per week (`stats/YYYY-MM-DD.md`):

| Post | Type | Hook style | Plays | Likes | Saves | Shares | Comments |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- Saves and shares tell you what's useful; plays tell you what hooked.
- Compare hook styles, not just posts. In our data, hook-led carousels beat product-led ones about ten to one.
- Platforms differ: carousels won on TikTok, reels won on Instagram. Let your own numbers decide.
