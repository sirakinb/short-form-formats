# Reference intake

Most good pieces start from a reference that already works. Use the same process every time.

## The brief (one message, to yourself or your AI assistant)
```
Reference: <link>
Angle: <what excites me, or the feature to show>
Platform: TikTok carousel | Instagram reel | both
Talking head: <path to my take, or none>
```

## The process
1. **Save it** to `references/<slug>/` and add a row to your reference library (`references/INDEX.md`): date, link, creator, type, what to take from it, where you used it.
2. **Break it down:** a frame strip (reels) or contact sheet (carousels); for reels, the audio split with demucs; the hook, beat-by-beat structure, caption style, pace, palette and type.
3. **Check the claims** against what your product really does.
4. **Pick the format:** carousel → `formats/news-carousel.md`; reel that should feel like it → `formats/reference-remake.md`; your talking head attached → `formats/talking-head.md`.
5. **Script or slide list,** led by your angle.
6. **Build, review, post** (`posting-checklist.md`).
7. **Test and log** (`testing-and-stats.md`).

A reference library means a quiet week starts from a backlog, not a blank page.
