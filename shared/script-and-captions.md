# Script and captions

## Angle
- Start from what genuinely excites you about the topic, not a generic problem statement. "Agents from different apps can now talk to each other" beats "AI safety matters".
- Every product claim must come from the product's own site or docs. If it doesn't do something, don't say it does.

## Script
- **Hook in three layers:** what you say, the text on screen, and what the viewer sees, all in the first 2 seconds.
- **Length:** 30–75 s for reels. Cut the weakest beat if you're over.
- **Start mid-thought.** Never "hey guys" or "in this video".
- **Have a verdict.** Every script ends on an opinion, not a summary.
- Tell client stories in general terms ("a business", "you"), with no names or tells.

## Banned phrases (add yours in `brand.json`)
- "nobody's talking about" and its variants
- "here's exactly how", "here's what you need to know"
- On-screen "via" source tags and "tell me below" cards; credit sources in the caption.

## Caption
- 2–5 sentences that make sense without the video, ending on a question.
- 8–12 hashtags on their own line: 2–3 broad, the rest specific.
- Your CTA: your URL or "link in bio".

## CTA
- Default: "link in bio" or your URL on the end card.
- "Comment WORD and I'll send it" only works if a DM automation answers the comment. Without one, people comment, get nothing, and stop trusting the CTA.
