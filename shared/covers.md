# Covers

Every reel gets a 1080x1920 cover, and it matters: it is what your profile grid shows.

## Layout
- Key content inside the 3:4 grid crop: **y 240–1680**.
- An eyebrow label (mono, accent soft, wide letter-spacing), a big headline (3–9 words, sentence case), then one accent line.
- One visual that proves the headline: app icons with ticks, a big number, a timeline, a hit frame.
- Your URL in mono at the bottom.

## Kinds
| Kind | When |
|---|---|
| Graphic cover | Animated reels: reuse the reel's own parts in their end state (in Remotion, pass negative start times) |
| Photo cover | AI video or footage: a raw frame, a dark gradient, the reel's title |
| Talking-head cover | Your face and the hook |
| HTML cover | HyperFrames reels: one HTML page rendered to PNG |

## Checks
- No face under the headline. Push the photo down and fade its top edge into the background.
- Take photo frames from the raw clip, not the final render, so on-screen titles don't appear twice.
